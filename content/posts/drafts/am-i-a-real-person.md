I recently started playing _The Talos Principle: Reawakened_, and I
have to say, the themes of consciousness, person-hood, and artificial
intelligence hit differently in 2025 than they did in 2017(??) when I
first played the original release. A lot has changed in the world: the
rise of LLM "AI" agents, a second Trump Presidency, and the Republican
Party's assault against the rights of and safety of women, immigrants
and trans people. A lot has changed in my life: coming out as trans,
realizing how neurodivergent I actually am.

One of the pieces of lore in the game (very minor spoilers ahead),
appears to be the prompt for a writing assignment from a philosophy
class, which reads as follows.

> One day you wake up to discover that you are an artificial person,
> blah-blah, same capacities, your behavior is your own,
> etc. etc. (TODO! fill out real prompt from the game).

The prompt ends by asking a few leading questions:

> 1. How does this change your conception of yourself?
> 2. Does this change your conception of the world around you?
> 3. Do you tell anyone, or keep it secret? Why or why not?

I've been trying to write more because I'm applying to Oxide and I
~~hope~~ know I can produce better writing than I did [ten years
ago][old-blog-post], and given all these changes, I thought it might be
interesting to engage with this writing prompt.
