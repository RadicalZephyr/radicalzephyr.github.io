* Trajectory Planning

What is trajectory planning? Speaking super broadly and specifically
in the case of a 3D printer it's answering the question, how fast and
smoothly can we traverse the path that we've been given.

** What path?

The path comes from gcode files generated by slicer programs.

** How fast?

*** Why does it matter?

How fast directly translates into how long the print takes.

 - Better part integrity
 - Less energy
 - Less time for something to go wrong

*** How fast can we go?

Considerations from kinematics:

 - Can't start going instantly.
 - Can't stop instantly.
 - Can't change directions instantly.

Considerations from thermodynamics:

 - How fast can we heat plastic?
 - How fast can we feed the plastic?

*** It's not just about speed, it's about corners

** How smooth?

Why do we care?

 - So it's safe.
 - So it's correct.
 - So it's quiet.
